\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Controlled approximation of dual attention shows that sensitivity to the window size is strongly dataset-dependent: large on multi-organ CT, small on polyp and skin-lesion segmentation. With an appropriate window, ApproxDA-TransUNet improves Dice over DA-TransUNet on all three datasets. Attention approximation should be treated as a task-dependent design choice rather than an accuracy-neutral efficiency measure.

\section{Compliance with ethical standards}
\label{sec:ethics}

This study was conducted retrospectively using human subject data made available in open access by the Synapse~\cite{landman2015miccai}, Kvasir-SEG~\cite{jha2020kvasir}, and ISIC~2018~\cite{codella2019isic} challenges. Ethical approval was not required as confirmed by the licenses attached with the open access data.
